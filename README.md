# One Word for the Boss

One word that describes the boss: **Hoverer**.

Not the boss who shouts, not the boss who vanishes — the one who stands
behind your chair, watching your screen, breathing on your neck while you
type. Every office has one. Nobody has ever survived a hover.

## Entry

`main.ts` exports `main(): string`, which returns the single word
`"Hoverer"` (7 characters) after validating it: 1–30 characters, not
blank, a single word with no spaces.
